Do My Tools Need to Be in Perfect Condition?

No. We understand that tools get used — that's what they're for. We accept tools in used or worn condition, as long as they're in working order. A DeWalt drill with some scuffs and paint on it is still worth good money if it runs well.

The one thing we can't buy is broken or non-functioning tools. If your tool has a burnt-out motor, missing parts, or doesn't turn on, we won't be able to make an offer. But if it works, bring it in — we want to see it.

How Much Will I Get for My Power Tools?

Every offer is based on the specific tool, its condition, current resale demand, and whether you have the full kit. That said, here's a general idea of what drives value up:

  • Brand matters — Milwaukee, Snap-on, Hilti, and Festool hold value better than most
  • Batteries included — cordless tools without batteries are worth significantly less
  • Complete sets — full kits in original cases are worth more than loose tools
  • Newer models — recent-generation tools command better prices
  • Working condition — tools that run cleanly get the best offers

The best way to find out what your tools are worth is to bring them in. Our appraisals are free and there's no obligation — if you don't like the offer, you walk out with your tools. No hard sell, no pressure.

Yes — we'll buy batteries and chargers on their own, especially name-brand packs like Milwaukee M18, DeWalt 20V, or Makita 18V. Bring them in and we'll take a look.

Absolutely. If you want to keep your tools but need short-term cash, you can use them as collateral for a pawn loan. You get cash now, and when you're ready, you pay back the loan and get your tools back. It's a straightforward process with no credit check required.
