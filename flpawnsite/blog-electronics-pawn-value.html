What Is My Old iPhone, Laptop, or Gaming Console Worth at a Pawn Shop?

Pawn shop values depend on model, condition, and market demand — not what you paid. Here's exactly how we price electronics and how to get the most cash for yours.

If you've got an old iPhone sitting in a drawer, a laptop you upgraded from, or a gaming console collecting dust, you've probably wondered what it's actually worth. The honest answer: it depends on several factors — model, condition, accessories, and current resale demand. This guide breaks down how Florida Pawn values electronics and what you can do to maximize your offer.

Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) commands a strong premium because demand is consistent and reliability is high. For Windows laptops, an NVIDIA GPU, 16GB+ RAM, and an NVMe SSD matter most. A laptop with a cracked screen or dead battery will receive a reduced offer — but it's still worth bringing in; we work with what you have.

How to Prepare Your Device and Get the Best Offer

  • Factory reset your device — remove your Apple ID, Google account, or PlayStation/Xbox account before coming in. We can't accept a device that's still signed in.
  • Charge it up — a device that powers on and runs smoothly gets a better assessment than one that's dead.
  • Find the original accessories — chargers, cables, controllers, cases, even the original box if you have it.
  • Check if it's unlocked — for phones, carrier-unlocked devices are worth more and easier to resell.
  • Know the model — check Settings > About (iPhone) or the sticker on the bottom (laptops) for exact model information.
  • Be honest about the condition — we'll inspect it anyway. Accurate condition saves everyone time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment to sell or pawn my electronics?

No appointment needed. Walk in during business hours with your device and a valid ID. The appraisal is free and there's no obligation to accept our offer.

What if my phone is broken or cracked?

Bring it in anyway. A cracked screen or bad battery reduces the offer, but broken devices can still have value depending on the model and what's wrong with it.

Do you test electronics before making an offer?

Yes. All devices are tested on-site before we make an offer. This protects both of us and ensures the offer reflects the actual condition of the item.

Can I pawn my laptop and get it back later?

Yes — that's exactly what a pawn loan is for. We hold the item, you get cash. Repay the loan plus fees within the loan term and your laptop comes back to you. More on pawn loans here.

How do you determine the value of my device?

We check current resale prices on platforms like eBay and other secondary markets, then factor in condition, model, and accessories. Our offers reflect real market value, not a lowball guess.

What ID do I need to bring?

A valid government-issued photo ID — driver's license, state ID, or passport. This is required by Florida law for all pawn and sale transactions.
