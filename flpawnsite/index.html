Our Story

A Family Business Built on Trust

Florida Pawn is a family-run business with roots going back to 1947, when Martin Kaminsky returned from WWII service and opened a shop in Brooklyn — buying, selling, and repairing the timepieces he had learned to work with during the war.

His son Mitchell joined after earning his MBA, expanding the business into one of the largest pawnbroking operations in the New York metro area. After Mitchell's passing in 2017, the next generation of the Kaminsky family has continued that legacy.

Florida Pawn carries that same family tradition to Broward County. Our Hollywood and Margate locations operate on the same principles that have guided the business for generations: honest appraisals, fair prices, and genuine respect for every customer who walks through the door.
