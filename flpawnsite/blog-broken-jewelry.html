What Your Karat Marking Actually Means

Every piece of real gold jewelry is stamped with a karat marking — usually inside a ring band or on a clasp. That number tells you exactly how much pure gold is in the piece, and it's the single biggest factor in what we pay.

What About Stones in Broken Jewelry?

If your broken piece has diamonds or other gemstones, we evaluate those separately from the metal. A diamond in a broken ring is still a diamond — and depending on the stone's size and quality, it can add meaningfully to your offer. We look at the 4 Cs (cut, color, clarity, carat) and factor that into what we pay. Even small accent stones add up when there are several of them.

Semi-precious stones like sapphires, rubies, emeralds, and topaz are evaluated individually. Some add value; some are too small to affect the price much. Either way, we tell you exactly what we're paying for and why.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you buy gold-plated jewelry?

Gold-plated pieces have a very thin layer of gold over a base metal — usually not enough to have meaningful melt value. We'll test it and be straight with you. If it's plated, we'll tell you. If it's solid gold, we'll pay for it.

My jewelry has no stamp — can you still buy it?

Yes. We test every piece with acid and electronic verification regardless of whether there's a stamp. If it's real gold or silver, we'll know, and we'll make you an offer accordingly.

How do you calculate what you pay?

We weigh the piece, verify the karat, and apply the current gold spot price to calculate the melt value. Our offer reflects a strong percentage of that melt value — consistently higher than what you'd get from most competitors.

Can I get more if I repair the jewelry first?

In most cases, no. We pay based on metal content, not wearability. The cost of a repair almost always exceeds any difference it would make in our offer. Bring it broken — it makes no difference to us.

What if I want to pawn it instead of selling outright?

That's an option too. A pawn loan lets you use your jewelry as collateral for a short-term loan — you get cash now, and you can redeem the piece when you repay. Good option if you want to keep the jewelry but need cash quickly.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in anytime during business hours at either our Hollywood or Margate location. We'll evaluate your jewelry on the spot.
